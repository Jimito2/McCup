600McGill students on the field this year

Big leagues feel far away. McCup brings the game to your faculty, your residence, and your friends.

McCup runs short, small-scale competitions across campus. You don't need to be an athlete. You need a team, a free afternoon, and a reason to win.

Every event is built to be quick to join and easy to finish, so you can compete between classes and still be home for dinner.
